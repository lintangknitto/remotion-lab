Okay, let's talk about clipping.
Everyone thinks you need a fancy editor, but that is not true.
The thing that actually matters is the hook.
If the first three seconds are boring, nothing else saves you.
Here is what works.
Start with the most surprising sentence in the whole episode.
Cut everything before it.
Then add captions, because most people watch with the sound off.
A good caption is not a transcript, it is a rhythm.
Three or four words at a time, matching the beat of the voice.
And one more thing.
Do not clip the ending.
End on the punchline, then stop.
No outro, no subscribe reminder, no dead air.
If you do those three things, your clips will beat clips made with better cameras.
That is the whole workflow.
